About

A designer who builds, not just draws.

I'm Brajraj Singh Songara, a website designer focused on turning ideas into interfaces that feel effortless to use. Every project starts with the same question: what does this person need to feel, in the first three seconds?

From there — layout, type, motion, and color are chosen on purpose, never by default. The result is a site that looks premium, loads fast, and actually gets the click, the message, or the sale.

I work end-to-end: design, front-end build, and the small animated details that make a site feel alive.
